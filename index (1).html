Built as a standing reading resource for Marine officers to locate professional and historical titles quickly, on any device.

This is an independent reading-collection tool and is not an official system of the Armed Forces of the Philippines, the Philippine Navy, or the Philippine Marine Corps. The catalog is maintained through the Manage Collection panel — no coding required.
